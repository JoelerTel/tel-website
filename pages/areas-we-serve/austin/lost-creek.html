Expert Electrical Services for Lost Creek

From modernizing 1970s electrical panels to illuminating mature oak canopies to installing whole-home generators, we provide specialized services for Lost Creek established luxury properties.

Panel Upgrades and Electrical Modernization

Lost Creek established homes often have original 100-150 amp panels struggling with today's demands. We upgrade panels safely and efficiently, giving your home the capacity it needs.

  • 200-400 amp panel upgrades for homes with growing electrical needs
  • Replacement of outdated Federal Pacific and Zinsco panels
  • Aluminum wiring assessment and remediation common in 1970s homes
  • GFCI and AFCI protection upgrades for safety and code compliance
  • Circuit additions for EV chargers, home offices, and modern appliances
  • Whole-house surge protection

Generator Installation and Backup Power

Lost Creek heavily wooded setting means storm-related outages are a regular occurrence. A whole-home generator protects your family comfort, preserves food and medications, and keeps well pumps running.

  • Whole-home standby generators properly sized for larger homes
  • Automatic transfer switches for seamless power transitions
  • Battery backup alternatives including Tesla Powerwall
  • Generator placement that respects Lost Creek quiet character
  • Annual maintenance programs for reliable operation
  • Critical circuit backup for essential systems
