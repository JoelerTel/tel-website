Panel Upgrades & Whole-Home Rewiring

Many West Lake Hills homes built in the 1960s-1990s have electrical systems that were adequate when built but can't meet modern demands. Original 100-150 amp panels, aluminum wiring, and undersized circuits are common issues we address. Whether you need a simple panel upgrade or complete rewiring, we have the expertise to modernize your home's electrical system safely.

Generator Installation & Backup Power

West Lake Hills' beautiful wooded hillsides come with a trade-off: power outages from storms are common. Many homeowners rely on generators to protect their families during extended outages, keeping refrigerators running, HVAC operating, and home offices functional. We install both whole-home standby generators and critical-circuit systems.

  • Whole-home standby generators (Generac, Kohler)
  • Critical-circuit backup systems
  • Automatic transfer switches
  • Battery backup systems (Tesla Powerwall)
