Panel Upgrades & Service Expansion

Most original Rollingwood homes have 100-150 amp electrical panels—completely inadequate for modern kitchens, HVAC systems, EV chargers, and home offices. Panel upgrades are often the first step in any significant renovation, providing the capacity foundation for everything else.

  • 200-400 amp service upgrades
  • Main panel replacement with modern breakers
  • Subpanels for additions and outbuildings
  • Austin Energy coordination and inspection

Whole-Home Rewiring

Many Rollingwood homes being renovated need complete rewiring. Original 1950s-1980s wiring often includes aluminum conductors, cloth-insulated wire, or undersized circuits that simply can't meet modern demands or safety standards. During renovation is the perfect time to address everything.

  • Complete replacement of original wiring
  • Modern copper conductors throughout
  • GFCI and AFCI protection to current code
  • Proper circuit allocation for modern loads
