Your Trusted Electrician for the UT Austin Area

Texas Electric and Light has been serving the University of Texas area since 2004, providing reliable electrical services for one of Austin's most unique and high-demand markets. From aging rental houses near North Campus to modern high-rises in West Campus, we understand the full spectrum of electrical needs in this densely populated, fast-moving area.

The UT area presents electrical challenges unlike anywhere else in Austin — a mix of 1940s-1960s rental housing running on outdated panels, modern condo towers with complex building systems, active small businesses along The Drag, and faculty and staff homes requiring dependable, high-quality electrical work. We know this neighborhood and we're ready to help.

Rental Property Electrical Compliance & Safety

UT-area landlords face strict City of Austin rental inspection requirements and high liability exposure from aging electrical systems. We help property owners stay compliant, protect their investment, and keep tenants safe — quickly and efficiently.

  • City of Austin rental inspection compliance
  • GFCI outlet installation in kitchens, bathrooms & outdoor areas
  • Smoke detector and safety device wiring
  • Multi-unit property electrical maintenance
  • Pre-lease electrical inspections to protect landlord liability

Panel Upgrades for Aging Housing Stock

Many UT-area homes were built in the 1940s–1960s and are still running on 60–100 amp service — wholly inadequate for modern student living with multiple devices, gaming setups, appliances, and home offices. We upgrade panels safely and efficiently with minimal disruption.

  • 100A to 200A service upgrades
  • Federal Pacific and Zinsco panel replacements
  • Aluminum wiring remediation
  • Circuit additions for modern electrical loads
  • Outlet additions for high-density occupancy
Learn more about panel upgrades →
