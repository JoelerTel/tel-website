Frequently Asked Questions

What areas around Austin do you serve?

We serve the entire Greater Austin metropolitan area, including all neighborhoods within Austin city limits and surrounding communities such as Dripping Springs (our home base), Westlake, West Lake Hills, Lake Travis, Lakeway, Round Rock, Cedar Park, Georgetown, Pflugerville, Leander, Buda, Kyle, San Marcos, and Bee Cave. Our service radius extends approximately 50 miles from our Dripping Springs location.

Do you charge extra for service in certain areas?

We provide consistent, transparent pricing throughout our service area. While there may be a small travel fee for locations at the outer edges of our service radius, we're always upfront about all costs before beginning any work. Most areas within 30 miles of Dripping Springs have no additional travel charges.

How quickly can you respond to my neighborhood?

Response times depend on location and current demand. For emergency services: Dripping Springs area (15-25 minutes), Central Austin/Westlake/Lake Travis (25-35 minutes), and outer suburbs (35-50 minutes). For scheduled appointments, we offer specific time windows and pride ourselves on punctuality. Same-day service is often available for non-emergency work.

Are you familiar with the electrical codes in my area?

Absolutely! Our licensed electricians (TECL #28315) are thoroughly familiar with electrical codes throughout the Greater Austin area, including specific municipal and county variations. We stay current on all code changes and work closely with local inspectors to ensure your electrical work meets or exceeds all safety requirements and passes inspection on the first try.

Do you provide specialized services for my neighborhood's housing type?

Yes! We understand that different neighborhoods have unique housing types and electrical needs. From historic homes in Hyde Park requiring knob-and-tube replacement to luxury smart homes in Westlake needing advanced automation, from new construction in Dripping Springs to waterfront properties at Lake Travis requiring dock wiring - we provide tailored electrical solutions specific to your home's characteristics and neighborhood requirements.

What makes you different from other Austin area electricians?

As a locally-owned company based in Dripping Springs, we have deep roots in the community and genuine local expertise. We focus on four core services (EV chargers, panel upgrades, wiring, and safety inspections) that we excel at, rather than trying to be everything to everyone. Our neighborhood-specific knowledge, transparent pricing, quality guarantee, and commitment to customer service set us apart from larger, impersonal electrical companies.

Need Electrical Service in Your Neighborhood?

Contact us for fast, reliable electrical service from licensed professionals who know your area's unique electrical needs and building characteristics. From emergency repairs to planned upgrades, we're your trusted local electrical contractor.
