Service Hours and Rates

Standard Service Hours

Our standard service hours are Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Services performed during these hours are billed at our standard rates.

After-Hours Service

After-hours services (between 5:00 PM and 8:00 AM on weekdays, weekends, and holidays) are available but may incur additional charges. After-hours rates will be clearly communicated before service begins.

Emergency Services

Emergency services are available 24/7. Emergency service rates apply to:

  • Power outages
  • Electrical hazards
  • Burning odors from electrical systems
  • Sparking or smoking electrical components

Scheduling and Cancellations

We require 24-hour notice for service cancellations. Late cancellations or no-shows may result in a service fee.

For emergency services, we strive to respond within 60-90 minutes of your call.

Estimates and Pricing

Written estimates are provided for all major electrical work. Estimates are valid for 30 days unless otherwise specified.

Additional charges may apply if:

  • Additional problems are discovered during service
  • Access to work areas is restricted
  • Special equipment or materials are required
  • After-hours or emergency service is needed

Payment Terms

Payment is due upon completion of service unless other arrangements have been made in advance. We accept:

  • Cash
  • Credit/Debit Cards
  • Checks (for established customers)

For larger projects:

  • 50% deposit may be required to schedule work
  • Progress payments for extended projects
  • Final payment due upon completion

Permits and Inspections

  • Pay permit fees (if not included in estimate)
  • Provide access for inspections
  • Cooperate with inspection requirements

All work is performed to meet or exceed current electrical codes.

Work Authorization
