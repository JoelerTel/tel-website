Austin Panel Upgrade Options

100 to 200 Amp Upgrade

The most common upgrade for homes in Austin, especially homes built before 2000. A 200 amp panel provides ample power for modern appliances, HVAC systems, and electric vehicle charging.

Ideal for: Most single-family homes, homes with electric appliances, properties with pools or EV chargers.

Fuse Box to Circuit Breaker Conversion

Many older homes in Austin neighborhoods like Hyde Park and Clarksville still have fuse boxes. We replace these outdated systems with modern circuit breakers that provide better safety and convenience.

Ideal for: Historic homes, properties built before 1960, homes with recurring electrical issues.

Sub-Panel Installation

For homes that need additional circuits for specific areas like workshops, garage conversions, or home additions. A sub-panel provides targeted electrical capacity without a full system upgrade.

Ideal for: Home additions, garage workshops, pool equipment, outdoor living spaces.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if I need a panel upgrade?

Signs you may need an upgrade include frequent circuit breaker trips, flickering lights, using multiple power strips, or if your panel is over 20 years old. Homes with less than 200 amp service may also benefit from an upgrade, especially if you're adding new appliances, an EV charger, or experiencing electrical issues.

How long does a panel upgrade take?

A typical panel upgrade takes 1-2 days to complete, including inspection. We handle all permits and coordinate with the city for a smooth process. During the installation, power may be temporarily off for 4-8 hours.

What size electrical panel do I need?

Most modern homes require 200 amp service. Factors include home size, major appliances, HVAC systems, and whether you have or plan to add an EV charger or pool. Older homes often have 100 amp or less, which is typically insufficient for today's electrical demands.

How much does a panel upgrade cost in Austin?

Panel upgrade costs in Austin typically range from $1,200 to $3,500 depending on your current panel, the amperage needed, and any additional work required. Upgrading from a fuse box to circuit breakers or from 100 to 200 amps will affect pricing. We provide free estimates before beginning any work.

Do I need permits for a panel upgrade in Austin?

Yes, electrical panel upgrades in Austin require permits and inspections to ensure code compliance. As licensed electricians (TECL #28315), we handle all permitting requirements and coordinate with city inspectors, making the process seamless for you.

Will a panel upgrade lower my electric bill?

While a panel upgrade primarily improves safety and capacity rather than efficiency, it can indirectly reduce electricity costs by eliminating inefficiencies from overloaded circuits. Modern panels distribute power more effectively, potentially reducing energy waste from outdated equipment.
