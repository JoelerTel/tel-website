Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can you respond to service requests?

Our response time varies based on your location and the nature of your request. For emergencies, we typically respond within 30-90 minutes. For standard service requests, we can usually schedule appointments within 24-48 hours in our primary service areas (Dripping Springs, Westlake, and Lake Travis).

Do you provide free estimates?

Yes, we provide free estimates for all electrical projects. You can request an estimate through our contact form or by calling us directly at (512) 547-4130. For most standard services, we can often provide estimates over the phone.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes, Texas Electric and Light is fully licensed (TECL #28315) and carries comprehensive insurance, including general liability and workers' compensation. We're committed to maintaining the highest standards of safety and professionalism on every job.

What types of payment do you accept?

We accept multiple payment methods including credit cards, checks, and cash. We can also discuss financing options for larger electrical projects. Payment terms will be clearly communicated before work begins.

Need Immediate Electrical Help?

Our licensed team is available 24/7 for emergency electrical service throughout the Greater Austin area. Don't wait when safety is at risk.
