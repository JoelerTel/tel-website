Frequently Asked Questions

What areas around Austin do you serve?

We serve the entire Greater Austin area, including all neighborhoods within Austin city limits and surrounding communities such as Dripping Springs, Westlake, Lake Travis, Round Rock, Cedar Park, Georgetown, Pflugerville, Buda, Kyle, and San Marcos.

Do you charge extra for service in certain areas?

We provide consistent pricing throughout our service area. While there may be a small travel fee for locations further from our Dripping Springs base, we're transparent about all costs before beginning any work.

How quickly can you respond to my neighborhood?

Response times vary by location and current demand. For emergency services, we typically arrive within 15-30 minutes in Dripping Springs, 30-45 minutes in Central Austin and Lake Travis areas, and 45-60 minutes in other suburbs. For scheduled appointments, we offer specific time windows and pride ourselves on punctuality.

Are you familiar with the electrical codes in my area?

Yes! Our licensed electricians are thoroughly familiar with electrical codes throughout the Greater Austin area, including specific municipal and county variations. We stay up-to-date on all code changes to ensure your electrical work is compliant and safe.

Do you provide specialized services for my neighborhood's housing type?

Absolutely. We understand that different neighborhoods have different housing types and electrical needs. From historic homes in Hyde Park to luxury estates in Westlake to new construction in Dripping Springs, we provide tailored electrical solutions specific to your home's characteristics and your neighborhood's requirements.
