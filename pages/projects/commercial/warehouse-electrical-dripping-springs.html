Commercial Ground-Up In Progress

40,000 SF Warehouse Complete Electrical

Dripping Springs, TX In Progress - 2025 Fitzhugh/Trautwein Area

Project Overview

This 40,000 square foot warehouse in the Fitzhugh and Trautwein Road area of Dripping Springs is being built from the ground up. Texas Electric and Light is handling the complete electrical installation - from the 400 amp service entrance to the final outlet and light fixture.

Ground-up construction gives us the opportunity to install the electrical system right the first time, with proper planning for the building's intended use and potential future expansion. We're working closely with the general contractor to coordinate rough-in, inspections, and trim-out phases.
