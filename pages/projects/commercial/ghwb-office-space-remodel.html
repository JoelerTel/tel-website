Project Overview

Texas Electric and Light is handling the complete electrical scope for a commercial office space remodel for GHWB in Austin. Office remodels present a unique set of electrical challenges — working in an occupied or recently vacated building, upgrading systems without full demolition, and delivering a modern, professional office environment that supports today's work patterns.

This project involves upgrading the existing electrical infrastructure to support a redesigned office layout — new workstation circuits, updated lighting throughout, conference room audio-visual power, and the kind of clean, organized electrical installation that a professional firm expects in their workspace.

We are coordinating with the GC through both demolition and buildout phases, ensuring electrical work stays ahead of finishes and the project delivers on schedule for GHWB's team to move in.

Scope of Work

Panel & Service Upgrades

  • • Existing panel evaluation and upgrades as needed
  • • New circuits for redesigned office layout
  • • Circuit labeling and panel organization
  • • Surge protection for office electronics
  • • Load balancing across all circuits

Office Lighting Upgrades

  • • LED lighting upgrade throughout office
  • • Dimmable lighting in conference rooms
  • • Task lighting circuits at workstations
  • • Reception and lobby accent lighting
  • • Emergency and exit lighting throughout

Workstation & Technology Power

  • • Dedicated workstation circuit layout
  • • Under-floor or in-wall power distribution
  • • Multi-outlet power columns and floor boxes
  • • IT room and server closet dedicated circuits
  • • UPS and battery backup power infrastructure

Conference & Meeting Rooms

  • • AV system power and dedicated circuits
  • • Display and projection screen power
  • • Conference table power and connectivity
  • • Video conferencing system electrical
  • • Dimmable lighting control per room

Common Areas & Support

  • • Kitchen and break room circuits
  • • Restroom electrical upgrades
  • • Reception desk and front-of-office power
  • • Security and access control electrical
  • • HVAC and thermostat electrical updates

GC Coordination & Completion

  • • Coordination with GC through demolition and rebuild
  • • Phased work to minimize disruption
  • • Rough-in ahead of drywall and finishes
  • • Trim-out after paint and flooring complete
  • • Final testing, labeling, and client walkthrough

Project Considerations

Remodel Phasing Without Full Demo

Office remodels often involve working around existing infrastructure rather than starting from scratch. We assess what to reuse, what to upgrade, and how to integrate new circuits into the existing system without unnecessary disruption to the building.

Modern Office Power Density

Today's offices require more circuits per square foot than older office buildings were designed to provide. Dual monitors, docking stations, task lighting, and always-on devices at every workstation add up quickly. The circuit layout has to anticipate actual usage patterns.

Schedule Coordination

Office remodels have tight schedules — the client's team needs to move in on a specific date, and every other trade is working toward the same deadline. Our electrical scope has to stay ahead of the critical path without creating bottlenecks for finishes.
