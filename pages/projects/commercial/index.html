Commercial Electrical Projects

Texas Electric and Light has completed commercial electrical work across Central Texas — from ground-up restaurant buildouts and EV infrastructure installations to full office renovations and tenant improvements. Every project is managed by our licensed team (TECL #28315) with a focus on code compliance, coordination with general contractors, and hitting your schedule.

We work with property owners, general contractors, and developers on projects ranging from 2,000 to 100,000+ square feet throughout Austin, Dripping Springs, Georgetown, Round Rock, and the surrounding area. Whether you need a competitive bid on a new buildout or a reliable electrical partner for your next tenant improvement, we'd like to hear about your project.
