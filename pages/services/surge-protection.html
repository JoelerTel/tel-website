Surge Protection in Austin

Why Austin Needs Surge Protection

Austin's climate and electrical infrastructure create unique surge protection challenges:

  • High Lightning Activity: Central Texas experiences some of the highest rates of lightning strikes in the country, particularly during our spring and summer thunderstorms.
  • Grid Vulnerabilities: Austin Energy's grid experiences fluctuations during peak demand periods, especially during extreme weather events like summer heat waves.
  • Hill Country Risk: Homes in Westlake, Lake Travis, and Dripping Springs are at higher risk due to elevated terrain and less urban infrastructure to diffuse lightning strikes.
