Common Questions

What kinds of landscape lighting do you install?

Path lights, spotlights, up-lights for trees and architectural features, driveway lights, step and stair lights, patio and pergola lighting, string lights on outdoor structures, and security lighting. We work with both low-voltage LED systems and standard 120V outdoor circuits.

Do I need a permit for outdoor lighting in Austin?

Low-voltage landscape lighting (12V transformer systems) generally does not require a permit. New 120V outdoor circuits or significant panel changes do. We handle the permit process when it applies.

Can you help design the layout?

Yes. We'll walk the property with you, talk through what you want to highlight, and suggest fixture placement and types before we quote. We're happy to work from your plans or start fresh.

Do you install smart or automated outdoor lighting?

Yes. We can wire outdoor lighting to smart switches, dimmers, timers, or photocell controls so lights turn on and off automatically at dusk and dawn or on a schedule.
