Common Questions

What indoor lighting work do you do?

We install and replace recessed lights, chandeliers and pendants, ceiling fans, undercabinet lighting, dimmers and smart switches, track lighting, bathroom vanity and exhaust fan fixtures, and commercial interior lighting. We also add new circuits when you want lighting where there is none.

Can you hang a chandelier in a two-story entryway?

Yes. High-ceiling installations are a regular part of our work. We set the fixture at the right height for the space and make sure the electrical box is properly rated for the weight.

Do I need a permit for interior lighting upgrades?

Replacing a fixture on an existing circuit generally does not require a permit. Adding new circuits, moving wiring, or making changes that involve the panel do. We handle permits and inspections with the City when they apply.

Can you install dimmers and smart switches?

Yes. We install standard dimmers, three-way dimmers, and smart switches compatible with Lutron Caseta, Leviton Decora, and similar systems. We'll make sure the wiring is correct for the switch you choose.
