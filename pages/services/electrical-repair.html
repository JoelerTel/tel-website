What We Work On

Residential and commercial electrical work throughout Austin. If it's electrical, we can likely help — here are the most common requests.

Electrical Repairs

Tripping breakers, dead outlets, flickering lights, burning smells, and other wiring issues. We diagnose and fix the root cause.

Outlets & Switches

Outlet replacements, GFCI upgrades, switch replacements, adding new outlets, USB outlets, and smart switch installations.

Circuit Work

Adding dedicated circuits for appliances, home offices, workshops, and equipment. Circuit repairs, tracing, and breaker replacements.

Wiring

Rewiring older homes, running new circuits for renovations, additions, ADUs, garages, and backyard structures.

Safety Inspections

Pre-purchase and pre-sale electrical inspections, home safety assessments, and documentation for insurance or real estate transactions.

Commercial Work

Tenant buildouts, lighting upgrades, panel work, circuit additions, and general electrical maintenance for offices, retail, and restaurants.

Common Questions

Do you work on homes and commercial buildings?

Yes. We work on residential homes, rental properties, small businesses, retail, office, and light commercial buildings throughout the Austin area.

How does your process work?

We start with a free on-site estimate. We assess the problem, give you a written scope and quote, then schedule the work. No repairs happen until you've approved the quote.

Do you handle permits?

Some electrical work in Austin requires a permit; some does not. We'll tell you upfront whether a permit is required for your job and handle the application if so.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Texas Electric and Light is a licensed electrical contractor (TECL #28315), insured, and bonded. We've been in business in the Austin area since 2004.
