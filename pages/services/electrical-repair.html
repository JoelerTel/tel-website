When to Call for Emergency Repair

Some electrical issues require immediate attention to prevent fire hazards or electrical injuries. Contact us immediately if you notice:

  • Sparks from outlets or panels
  • Complete power loss
  • Electric shock from appliances
  • Smoke or burning smell from outlets
  • Flooded electrical appliances/outlets
  • Hot electrical panel
