Signs You Need Outlet & Switch Upgrades

Watch for these warning signs that indicate your outlets and switches may need professional attention:

Safety Concerns

  • Sparking when plugging in devices
  • Burning smell around outlets or switches
  • Discoloration or scorch marks
  • Hot to the touch outlets or switch plates
  • No GFCI outlets in wet locations

Functionality Issues

  • Loose plugs that fall out of outlets
  • Switches that feel "sticky" or unresponsive
  • Frequent tripping of circuit breakers
  • Two-prong outlets needing three-prong upgrades
  • Not enough outlets for your devices

Austin-Specific Warning Signs

Austin homes face unique electrical challenges due to our climate and housing stock:

  • Outlets that stop working during summer AC usage (sign of overloaded circuits common in Austin's hot months)
  • Outdoor outlets with visible moisture damage from Austin's occasional heavy rains
  • Aluminum wiring in older Hyde Park and Travis Heights homes (requires specialized outlet installation)

Austin Electrical Code Compliance

All of our outlet and switch installations comply with the latest Austin electrical codes and National Electrical Code (NEC) standards. Our licensed electricians (TECL #28315) ensure your electrical system meets all safety requirements.

Austin GFCI Requirements

The City of Austin follows the National Electrical Code (NEC) requirements for GFCI protection, which includes:

  • Kitchens: All countertop outlets and within 6 feet of sink
  • Bathrooms: All outlets
  • Outdoor: All outlets must be weather-resistant GFCI
  • Garages, utility rooms, and unfinished basements
  • Pool areas and wet bars

Permit Requirements

The City of Austin typically requires permits for:

  • Adding new circuits for outlets or switches
  • Extensive rewiring projects
  • Major electrical renovations

For simple outlet or switch replacements (like-for-like), permits are generally not required. Our team will handle all necessary permits and inspections for your project.
