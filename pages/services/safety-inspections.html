Signs You Need an Electrical Inspection

Don't wait for a major problem to occur. Here are key warning signs that indicate your Austin home or business needs a professional electrical inspection:

Electrical Issues

  • Frequent circuit breaker trips
  • Dimming/flickering lights
  • Buzzing sounds from outlets
  • Burning smell near outlets

Property Situations

  • Home is over 25 years old
  • Purchasing/selling property
  • Recent renovation/addition
  • Storm or lightning damage

Austin-Specific Issues

  • AC system overloading circuit
  • Hill Country lightning exposure
  • Older knob & tube wiring (Hyde Park)
  • Pool/dock wiring near Lake Travis

Don't Ignore These Danger Signs

If you notice any of the following, call us immediately at (512) 547-4130 for an emergency inspection:

  • Visible sparks from outlets or switches
  • Warm or hot outlets, switches, or electrical panels
  • Electrical shocks when touching appliances or switches
