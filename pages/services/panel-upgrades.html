Common Questions

Do you handle permits for panel upgrades in Austin?

Yes. We handle the permit application, all required inspections, and coordination with the City of Austin on every panel upgrade. You don't have to deal with the city directly.

How long does a panel upgrade take?

Most residential panel upgrades are completed in one day. The scope depends on your current panel size, the upgrade you need, and any associated wiring work. We'll give you a clear timeline in your written quote.

Do I need a panel upgrade?

Common signs include frequently tripped breakers, a panel over 25 years old, adding high-draw appliances (EV chargers, hot tubs, HVAC), or buying a home with a fuse box or Federal Pacific panel. We'll assess your panel during the free estimate visit.

Do you work on commercial properties?

Yes. We handle panel upgrades and service increases for residential homes and commercial properties throughout the Austin area.

Will my power be out during the upgrade?

Yes — the power to your home or the affected circuits will be off during the upgrade. We coordinate the outage window and work to minimize downtime. Most residential jobs restore power the same day.
