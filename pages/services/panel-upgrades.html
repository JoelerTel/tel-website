Signs Your Austin Home Needs a Panel Upgrade

Safety Concerns

  • Breakers that frequently trip
  • Flickering or dimming lights
  • Warm or hot panel surface
  • Buzzing sounds from outlets or panel
  • Burning smell near electrical components

Capacity Issues

  • Fuse box instead of circuit breakers
  • Panel rated under 200 amps
  • Home built before 1990
  • Adding an EV charger or major appliance
  • Extensive use of power strips or extension cords

Austin-Specific Warning Signs

Summer AC Issues: If your panel struggles during Austin's hot summer months when AC usage is high, it's a clear indication you need more capacity.

Storm-Related Problems: Frequent power fluctuations during Austin's thunderstorm season may indicate inadequate protection in older panels.

Older Austin Neighborhoods: Homes in established areas like Hyde Park, Clarksville, or Travis Heights often have original electrical systems that are decades old.

Home Office Overload: Austin's growing remote work population is putting new demands on residential electrical systems.

Austin Panel Upgrade Options

100 to 200 Amp Upgrade

The most common upgrade for homes in Austin, especially homes built before 2000. A 200 amp panel provides ample power for modern appliances, HVAC systems, and electric vehicle charging.

Ideal for: Most single-family homes, homes with electric appliances, properties with pools or EV chargers.

Fuse Box to Circuit Breaker Conversion

Many older homes in Austin neighborhoods like Hyde Park and Clarksville still have fuse boxes. We replace these outdated systems with modern circuit breakers that provide better safety and convenience.

Ideal for: Historic homes, properties built before 1960, homes with recurring electrical issues.

Sub-Panel Installation

For homes that need additional circuits for specific areas like workshops, garage conversions, or home additions. A sub-panel provides targeted electrical capacity without a full system upgrade.

Ideal for: Home additions, garage workshops, pool equipment, outdoor living spaces.

200 to 400 Amp Upgrade

For larger luxury homes or properties with exceptionally high electrical demands. A 400 amp service provides abundant capacity for even the most electricity-intensive homes.

Ideal for: Large luxury homes in Westlake or Lake Travis, properties with multiple HVAC systems, homes with workshops, and extensive smart home technology.

Austin Electrical Code Compliance

All electrical panel upgrades in Austin must comply with the National Electrical Code (NEC) and local Austin amendments. Our licensed electricians ensure your panel upgrade meets all current code requirements, including:

  • Proper panel location and clearance requirements
  • AFCI and GFCI protection for required circuits
  • Proper grounding and bonding systems
  • Surge protection requirements for new installations
  • Required permits and inspections from Austin Development Services

As a licensed electrical contractor (TECL #28315), we handle all permitting and ensure your panel upgrade passes inspection the first time, saving you time and hassle.
