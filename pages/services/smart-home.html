Frequently Asked Questions

We install a wide range of smart home devices including smart switches, dimmers, outlets, thermostats, security cameras, doorbell cameras, smart locks, and whole-home automation systems. Our licensed electricians ensure all devices are properly wired and integrated with your existing electrical system for optimal performance and safety.

Many smart home devices work with existing electrical systems, but older Austin homes may need panel upgrades for optimal performance. We assess your current electrical capacity and recommend upgrades if needed. Smart home systems typically require neutral wires and stable power, which some older homes in areas like Hyde Park or Clarksville may lack.

Smart home installation costs vary based on the scope of work. Basic smart switch installations start around $299 for a few devices, while comprehensive whole-home automation systems can range from $2,500 to $8,999 depending on the size of your Austin home and features desired. We provide detailed quotes after assessing your specific needs and goals.
