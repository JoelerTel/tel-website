Signs You Need Lighting Upgrades

Electrical Issues

  • Flickering or dimming lights
  • Buzzing from light fixtures
  • Frequent bulb replacements
  • Circuit breakers tripping when lights are on

Efficiency Concerns

  • High electricity bills
  • Using outdated incandescent bulbs
  • Heat-generating fixtures in warm rooms
  • Lack of lighting controls or automation

Aesthetic Issues

  • Outdated fixture styles
  • Dark spots or uneven lighting
  • Poor lighting for tasks or activities
  • Unsafe or insufficient outdoor lighting
