Signs of an Electrical Emergency in Austin

Burning Smell

A burning plastic or rubber smell is often the first sign of overheating electrical components. This is especially common in older Austin homes with outdated wiring.

Sparking or Flames

Any visible sparking or flames from outlets, panels, or appliances requires immediate attention. Austin's hot climate can exacerbate electrical fire risks.

Frequent Circuit Breaker Trips

During Austin summer storms, power fluctuations can cause repeated circuit breaker trips, indicating potential overloads or short circuits.

Partial Power Loss

Common in Austin's older neighborhoods like Hyde Park and Clarksville, where partial power loss may indicate a dangerous problem with your electrical panel.

Water Near Electricity

During Austin's flash floods, water intrusion near electrical systems creates dangerous situations requiring immediate professional attention.

Buzzing or Humming Sounds

Consistent buzzing from outlets or panels is especially concerning in Lake Travis homes, where higher humidity can accelerate electrical deterioration.

Emergency Safety Tips

What to Do First

  • Stay away from electrical hazards and water
  • Turn off main power at the electrical panel if safe to do so
  • Keep everyone, including pets, clear of danger
  • Evacuate the building if you smell smoke or see fire
  • Call us immediately at (512) 547-4130

Have Ready

  • Location of your electrical panel
  • Detailed description of the problem
  • Safe access to your property
  • Information about any recent electrical work
  • Flashlight for navigating in case of outage
