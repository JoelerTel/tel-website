EV Charging in Austin, Texas

Austin leads Texas in electric vehicle adoption, with local initiatives supporting sustainable transportation. As a licensed electrical contractor (TECL #28315), we help Austin homeowners navigate local requirements for EV charger installations.

Local Rebates & Incentives

Austin Energy offers rebates up to $1,200 for residential EV charger installations when using a licensed electrician. Additional federal tax credits of up to 30% of installation costs (max $1,000) are available through the Inflation Reduction Act.
