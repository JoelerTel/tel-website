Why a Dedicated Circuit Makes a Difference

Many homeowners plug their EV charger into an existing outlet. It works — but it's slow and puts unnecessary load on shared circuits. Here's what a dedicated 240V circuit installed by a licensed electrician gives you:

  • ✓ Full charging speed. Your charger and vehicle can draw the full rated current without competing with other appliances on the same circuit.
  • ✓ No tripped breakers. A properly sized dedicated circuit won't trip when the charger runs overnight.
  • ✓ Code-compliant installation. The National Electrical Code requires a dedicated circuit for EVSE installations. We do it right so your panel and insurance are covered.
  • ✓ Ready for any charger. Whether you buy a Tesla, ChargePoint, Grizzl-E, Emporia, or another brand — a properly installed 240V circuit handles them all.

Common Questions

What is a dedicated circuit and why does it matter?

A dedicated circuit serves only your EV charger, giving it the full rated current without competing with other appliances. It's the right way to install a Level 2 charger — required by the National Electrical Code and essential for reliable, full-speed charging.

How much faster is a Level 2 charger?

A standard 120V outlet adds roughly 3–5 miles of range per hour. A Level 2 charger (240V) on a dedicated circuit can add 20–30+ miles per hour — often a full charge overnight instead of several days.

Do I need a permit for EV charger installation in Austin?

In most cases, yes — adding a dedicated circuit and Level 2 EVSE requires a permit in the City of Austin. We handle the permit application and inspection as part of the installation.

Can you install chargers at commercial or multi-family properties?

Yes. We install EV charging infrastructure at office buildings, retail, multi-family properties, and parking facilities throughout the Austin area. We'll assess panel capacity and design the right solution.
